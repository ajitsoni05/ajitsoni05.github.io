The real toy, in your hands

A ball with weight

The ball gathers speed, carries its momentum into the corners, clacks off the walls and teeters on the rims of the holes. The physics is worked out 1,200 times a second.

Every knock and roll has its own sound, and on iPhone you feel them too.

46 levels and a Daily Maze

From the Workshop to the Master Labyrinth

  • Start in the Workshop, then on through Arcade, Time Trial, the Classic Maze, Deep Wood, Precision and the Master Labyrinth.
  • Beat the gold time for three stars.
  • Collect green, blue and red gems. Take every gem on a level to double your points.
  • Checkpoints, keys and brass gates, and iris holes that open and close.

Make it yours

Balls and boards that look and sound of what they're made of

Balls: golf, eight ball, basketball, football, glass marble, gold, disco, lava and an energy orb.

Boards: maple, machined steel, ice, glass, neon and gold.

Pay for them with the gems you win on the boards.

Play your way

Race a friend

Race the autopilot, or a friend on another iPhone or iPad nearby, to the middle of a board for two.

Gentle or true to the toy

A beginner ball that stops when you hold still, or steel that rolls like the real thing and keeps going.

Tilt or drag

Steer by tilting, or by dragging a finger on the board. Sensitivity, dead zone and maximum tilt are yours to set.

For everyone

High contrast, a top-down camera and reduced motion. Checkpoints, keys and gems always have distinct shapes.

Made for iPad too

The whole board, on the big screen.
